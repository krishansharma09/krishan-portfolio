Personal Finance Dashboard

"Upload a bank CSV and instantly see intelligent spending insights — charts, summaries, and sortable tables."

01 Overview

Personal Finance Dashboard is a full-stack web application that transforms raw bank statement CSV files into actionable financial insights. Simply upload your exported CSV and the app auto-categorizes your transactions using keyword-based logic.

The dashboard presents summary cards for total income, total expenses, and net savings, paired with switchable chart types (bar, pie, line) powered by Chart.js, and a fully sortable and filterable transactions table — all built with a clean Flask + Pandas backend.
